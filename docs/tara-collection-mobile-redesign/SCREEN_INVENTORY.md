# Screen Inventory & Hierarchy

## 1. Primary Bottom Navigation Screens
| Tab | Screen Name | Key Mobile Capabilities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Billing (POS)** | Large search bar, category chips, product cards, custom item button, variant bottom sheet, cart sheet, sticky checkout bar. |
| 2 | **Stock** | Stock overview pills (Live, Low, Out), search & category filter, product cards with size/color stock chips, tap variant for stock adjustment sheet, Add Product wizard. |
| 3 | **Bills** | Mobile cash memo cards (Bill #, Date, Items, Discount, Paid), Quick Filters (Today, Yesterday, 7 Days, Month), View / Print Receipt sheet, WhatsApp share, Cancel action. |
| 4 | **More** | Clean grouped 2-column grid linking to: Dashboard, Purchases, Suppliers, Customers, Returns/Exchange, Expenses, Reports, Settings. |

## 2. Secondary Views Accessed via "More" Menu
- **Dashboard**: Compact KPI cards (Today's sales, bills, discounts, estimated profit), 7-day trend bar, category pie.
- **Purchase Entry**: Inward stock entry with Supplier selection, variant picker, and stock addition.
- **Suppliers**: Contact cards, purchase totals, outstanding balances, Add Supplier modal.
- **Customers**: Contact cards, bill counts, dues, Add Customer modal.
- **Returns & Exchange**: Find bill number, select items & condition (Good/Damaged), refund or exchange difference.
- **Expenses**: Daily & monthly shop expense recording (Rent, Electricity, Tea, Salaries).
- **Reports**: Net Sales, Discounts, Historical COGS Profit & Loss, CSV export.
- **Settings**: Shop profile, default ₹50 discount per piece, printer setup (80mm/58mm), backup & restore.
