# Mobile UX Plan — TARA COLLECTION Phone-First POS

## 1. Core Principles
- **One-Thumb Operation**: Key actions (Add to Bill, Continue to Payment, Print Slip) placed in the lower thumb zone (sticky bottom bars and bottom sheets).
- **Minimum Clicks to Bill**: A cashier can tap a product, tap Size, tap "Add to Bill", and see the sticky checkout bar update with instant ₹50/pc discount.
- **Hindi-Friendly Touch Labels**: Simple intuitive placeholders and micro-copy (e.g. *"Product search karein (Jeans, Shirt, Lower...)"*, *"Bina inventory ka item jodein"*).
- **Clean 4-Item Fixed Bottom Navigation**:
  1. **Billing** (Default POS screen)
  2. **Stock** (Products & inventory card view)
  3. **Bills** (Sales history & reprint)
  4. **More** (Drawer/Grid for Dashboard, Purchases, Customers, Suppliers, Returns, Expenses, Reports, Settings)

## 2. Touch-Friendly Target Specifications
- Minimum touch target: 44px × 44px.
- Primary CTA buttons: Full-width sticky buttons with 52px height, bold font, and high contrast.
- Size & Color chips: Large pill buttons (min 42px height) with active state highlighting.
- Numeric quantity adjustments: Large `+` and `-` buttons (40px × 40px) with central bold quantity badge.

## 3. Viewport Targets
- 360 × 800 (Compact Android)
- 375 × 812 (iPhone SE / Standard Android)
- 390 × 844 (Mainstream smartphones)
- 412 × 915 (Samsung Galaxy series)
- 430 × 932 (Large mobile displays)
- Responsive auto-scaling for iPad/Tablet and Desktop.
